EVALUATION / FIXED-SCOPE PROOF

Start with a
bounded question.

Does a repeatable, row-linked comparison help your team review a migration checkpoint more clearly than its current manual process?

01 / PROPOSED SCOPE

One pair of exports.
One defined review.

The initial evaluation is intentionally small. It tests the workflow before anyone discusses broader deployment or additional capabilities.

PROPOSED EVALUATIONMV / PILOT 01
01One source export and one target export
02One agreed unique comparison key
03Up to three selected fields
04CSV or flat-object JSONL inputs
05JSON result, HTML report and SHA-256 manifest
06Findings note separating observations from explanations
SCOPE, FEE, DELIVERY DATE AND TERMS MUST BE AGREED IN WRITING BEFORE WORK BEGINS.
02 / EVALUATION SEQUENCE

Safe sample first.

01

Qualify the workflow

Confirm an active migration checkpoint, a named reviewer, the export format, key and fields.

02

Use safe inputs

Start with synthetic or properly de-identified files. Do not send personal, confidential or regulated data through public channels.

03

Agree the engagement

Confirm scope, fee, delivery date, data handling, authorization and any license terms in writing before processing.

04

Review the result

Inspect exceptions together, document unresolved explanations and decide whether the workflow merits another project.

03 / ACCEPTANCE

Deliverables, not promises.

An evaluation is complete when the agreed inputs are parsed or limitations documented; the key is valid; each agreed field has a result; row references are present where available; and the agreed artifacts are delivered.

Important boundary

Acceptance means the scoped outputs were produced. It does not mean the migration is correct, every defect was detected, or the tool is certified for production use.

Data and legal arrangements

Migraven currently has no published commercial license or finalized customer data-processing agreement. Do not submit real customer data or assume commercial-use rights until the necessary terms, authorization and appropriate adult/contractual arrangements are in place.
