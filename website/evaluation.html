02

03

Inspect exceptions together, document unresolved explanations and decide whether repeat use is valuable.

Migraven has not yet published a commercial licence or finalized customer data-processing agreement. Real customer data should not be processed until appropriate legal, privacy, security and contractual arrangements are in place.
