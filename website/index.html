THE PROBLEM / THE OUTPUT

A migration can finish
before its evidence is clear.

Teams often end up with two exports, a spreadsheet of spot checks, and a sign-off decision that is hard to reconstruct later.

Migraven gives migration leads a repeatable way to compare the records and fields they have agreed to inspect. It identifies observed differences and one-sided records, then preserves the row references needed for a person to investigate.
