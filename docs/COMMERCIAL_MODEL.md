# Migraven commercial model

## Initial buyer

ERP/CRM implementation consultancies and migration delivery teams that run repeated migration projects and need a repeatable record-level verification step before client or internal sign-off.

## Buyer problem

Migration review often depends on ad hoc spreadsheet filters, manual spot-checks and disconnected evidence. Migraven offers a bounded alternative: compare two agreed exports using an explicit unique key and selected fields, then deliver an exception register with row references.

## Commercial sequence

### 1. Paid evaluation

A fixed-scope, paid evaluation should include:
- one source and one target export pair;
- one agreed unique key;
- up to three critical fields;
- agreed CSV or flat-object JSONL inputs;
- JSON comparison result, offline HTML report and SHA-256 manifest;
- a reviewer walkthrough and findings note.

Fee, scope, timeline, payment method, data handling and licence terms must be agreed in writing before work begins. Do not quote an unvalidated price as established market pricing.

### 2. Annual team licence

Offer only after the evaluation proves repeat use and the following exist:
- a published commercial licence;
- versioned releases and update policy;
- support scope and response expectations;
- secure distribution and checksum/signature policy;
- documented data handling and retention terms;
- procurement-ready contract terms.

### 3. Consultancy partner licence

A higher-value recurring offer for consultancies that want to standardise the verification workflow across multiple client projects. It requires explicit multi-project rights, deployment boundaries, support terms and restrictions on redistribution.

## Conversion design

The website should:
1. explain the buyer and the operational job;
2. show a working synthetic demo before asking for commitment;
3. make the method and limitations easy to inspect;
4. offer a bounded evaluation rather than an unsupported enterprise promise;
5. capture the organisation, work email and migration context;
6. move from evaluation to annual or partner licensing only after repeat value is established.

## Current commercial readiness

The website's enquiry form creates a local email draft; it does not send a message or collect leads server-side. There is no payment checkout, active subscription, validated public price, published commercial licence or customer data-processing agreement in this repository. Those are launch gates, not capabilities to imply.

Start initial demonstrations with synthetic or appropriately de-identified data. Real customer-data processing requires authorisation and suitable contractual, privacy and security controls.
